Most creators lose people in the first 2 seconds because their hook sounds optional.

Instead of asking:
"Do you want more views?"

Say:
"Your first 2 seconds are killing your views."

A question makes people decide if they care.
A bold statement makes them stop and react.

Use this on your next Reel:
1. Say the problem clearly
2. Make it specific
3. Create a little tension

Bad hook: "Want better captions?"
Better hook: "Your captions are making people swipe."

Save this before you film your next post.
