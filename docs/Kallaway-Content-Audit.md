## 2. The Kallaway method, distilled

### 2.1 Core operating beliefs

1. **Scientist, not artist.** Content decisions come from data (outliers,
   patterns, tests), not intuition. "Art is sexy, but science is how you
   actually build a business."
2. **Ideas > everything.** Most people spend the least time on the idea and the
   most on editing. Reverse it. A great idea is 100x more shareable.
3. **Hooks are the 80 of the 80/20.** Fixing hooks is the single biggest lever.
4. **On-target virality, not pure virality.** Views from the wrong audience
   train the algorithm wrong and convert at ~0. Want the biggest video that
   still fits your exact buyer.
5. **Algorithms want two numbers:** shares and average view duration (AVD).
   Everything else is noise. Metric priority: shares > comments > saves >
   likes > views.
6. **One platform, one format, one topic** until it runs 30 days without
   breaking. Then expand ("methodical expansion" — the opposite is "failure by
   suffocation").
7. **Volume with rising skill can't lose.** Get quality to 7/10 → max volume
   (5 shorts/week or 1 YT/week) → push quality to 9 → double volume.

### 2.2 Hooks (his deepest material)

- **Every hook needs exactly two things:** topic clarity + on-target curiosity.
- **Three-step spoken hook:** Context Lean → Scroll-Stop Interjection ("but…")
  → Contrarian Snapback. (His Vegas Sphere video, 8M views.)
- **Four components must align:** visual hook, spoken hook, text hook (3–5 word
  overlay), audio. Brain processes visual → audio → visual; the **key visual**
  matters more than the words. Misalignment = comprehension loss = death.
- **Six hook archetypes** (any topic can use any of them; pick the one with max
  contrast vs. your key visual): Fortune Teller (present vs. future),
  Experimenter (peer demo), Teacher (lesson), Magician (visual/spoken stun,
  visual pacifier), Investigator (uncovered secret), Contrarian (stated
  disagreement).
- **Desire-based hook** = the best format for turning views into leads:
  relatable character + dream outcome, with **zero conditions the viewer can't
  replicate**. Five mad-libs: "I just did X using Y" / "If I wanted X, I'd do
  Y" / "If you want X, use Y" / "Is it possible to X under Y?" / "This person
  just did X under Y." (His AI-tip reel: 21K leads, 42K comments.)
- **Four hook failure modes (diagnostic):** Delay (topic not in line 1),
  Confusion (not 6th-grade clarity, passive voice), Irrelevance (not "you/your",
  no pain point), Disinterest (no contrast → no curiosity).
- **One standard deviation rule:** never lead with the raw desire ("make more
  money" trips the BS detector); lead with the proxy pain point ("grow on
  social") and let the viewer complete the leap.
- Single subject, single question: 50K viewers should all be asking the same
  question after the hook.

### 2.3 Ideas & virality

- **Virality formula:** uncommon idea (or common idea + uncommon lens) + an
  unnaturally large applicable audience. Validate ideas by finding a version
  that already performed somewhere.
- **Idea mining loop (1 hr/week):** watch-list of 15–20 niche accounts → sort
  by **outlier score** (views ÷ channel average; 5x+ filter) → extract 7 Lego
  bricks per winner (topic, angle, hook, story structure, visual format, key
  visuals, audio) → build a superset → remix (Virgil Abloh's 3% rule: change
  3% and it's yours).
- **Change one Lego brick at a time** between reps — scientific method, not
  full reinvention.
- **Concentric circles / 40-40-20:** 40% core (buyer's problem in their niche),
  40% inner (same problem, tangential niches), 20% outer (same problem, any
  business), **0% max** (generic broad content — the viral trap).
- **Nine attributes:** getting attention = TAM resonance, idea explosivity
  (shock score 80–90/100 target), emotional magnitude, novelty; holding
  attention = speed to value, curiosity amplitude, absorption rate, rehook
  rate, stickiness.

### 2.4 Storytelling & scripts

- Expectations vs. reality is the whole game; the brain is a prediction
  machine — keep it off-balance.
- **Story loops:** context (open) → reveal (close). Shorts = 3–4 loops; YT =
  8–10. Pick the highest shock-score reveals first, then write context
  backwards.
- **But/Therefore, never And-Then** (Matt Stone/Trey Parker).
- **Rehook every 20–25s** (shorts) / every 60–90s or 3–4 min (YT). Loop-opener
  lines: "But that's not even the best part…", "Here's what nobody talks
  about…"
- **Story locks:** name your frameworks (term branding), embedded truths
  ("when" not "if" — kill hedging words), thought narration ("you're probably
  thinking…"), negative frames (loss aversion is 2x — "stop doing X" beats
  "do X"), contrast words (but/actually/instead/turns out).
- **Body point order:** 2nd-best point first, best second — ascending value
  keeps people in.
- **Last dab:** the final line should be the single most shareable sentence;
  in shorts it loops back into the hook.
- Sixth-grade vocabulary, active voice, jagged sentence lengths, one core idea
  per video ("frame the most interesting wedge and get out").

### 2.5 Packaging (YouTube) — for when we/clients go long-form

- Packaging = title + thumbnail + intro; "if they don't click, they don't
  watch." Same video went 1,700 → 1.2M views on a thumbnail change alone.
- Thumbnail: max 3 visual elements of his 7 stun-gun categories; design for
  dark mode (80%+ of viewers); test 3+ variants; intro must repeat the title
  promise in the first 4 seconds (click confirmation).
- Short-form packaging = first caption line + cover thumbnail + 3–5s hook.
  Custom covers matter for DM shares + profile binge conversion.

### 2.6 Business playbooks (client-facing gold)

- **The 2x2 strategy matrix:** personal brand vs. brand account × entertainment
  vs. education. Default to personal brand ("people buy from people"); pick
  entertainment when the product is self-explanatory (food, retail, consumer),
  education when the offer needs explaining (services, agencies, contractors).
- **7-step modern funnel:** problem aware → category aware → brand aware →
  solution aware → category education → solution education → conversion.
  Entertainment content covers steps 3–4; education covers 5–6. Skip steps the
  buyer already has embedded.
- **The ramp:** validated CTA → comment keyword → ManyChat DM → lead magnet →
  email → 5-day drip. ≤$300 products convert on DM alone; ≤$2K need the drip;
  >$2K need longer nurture.
- **Batch testing:** 10 videos per batch, 3 stacks, hold 4 of 5 Lego bricks
  constant, look for 10x outliers, carry winners forward. 50–100 videos to
  find 3 validated formats.
- **SuperUGC / echo effect:** 5–20 small creators ($300/mo B2C, $1K/mo B2B)
  posting derivative-branded accounts with affiliate links; ~1 in 40 videos
  goes viral; winning creative becomes paid ads.
- **TV network strategy for brands:** main account as landing page + hidden R&D
  account for zero-risk testing + spinout accounts for every format that wins
  3+ times.
- **Trust equation:** Trust = minutes × relevance × usefulness. Five levers:
  more videos, longer videos, more bingeable, more relevant, more useful.
- **Creator money ladder:** CPMs → UGC → brand deals → owned products. Real
  money is in owned; don't start there.
